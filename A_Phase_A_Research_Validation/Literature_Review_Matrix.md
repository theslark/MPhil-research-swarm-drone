# Literature Review Matrix (Preliminary, Phase A)

**Title:** AI-Driven Collaborative Environment Mapping, Resilient Coordination, and Self-Healing in Decentralized UAV Swarms under Intermittent Connectivity

**Status:** Preliminary. Compiled 2026-11-23.

**Verification.** Every row below was checked against its Crossref DOI record, and the abstract was retrieved from OpenAlex. Full bibliographic fields are in `Reference_Verification_Table.csv`, where IDs `Lxx` match this table. All 46 rows are peer-reviewed journal articles or conference papers published 2023–2026. Rows marked *(review)* or *(survey)* are peer-reviewed but are not primary studies. Neither group includes preprints.

**Evidence level.** The **Dataset**, **Method**, and **Main Findings** columns come only from each paper's abstract. Full texts have **not** been read yet. Reading them is a Phase D task, needed before any finding can be used in the paper's argument.

**Limitations column**
- **A:** a limitation the authors state in the abstract. "A: n/s" means the abstract does not state one, so the full text must be checked.
- **C:** my own critical assessment relative to this research.

Themes: **A** decentralized swarm systems · **B** collaborative mapping · **C** shared environmental intelligence · **D** communication resilience · **E** self-healing / fault tolerance · **F** AI and multi-agent coordination.
